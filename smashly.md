# Privacy Policy for Smashly

**Last Updated: September 14, 2026**

## Privacy Policy

Welcome to Smashly. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Smashly collects, uses, and protects your information.

## Information We Collect

Smashly is designed to help badminton players keep score, track match history, and reference the rules of the game — all without an account or sign-up.

The app stores the following information locally on your device:

- Match data you create, including game type (singles/doubles), format (best of 1/3/5), team or player names you enter, scores, and match results.
- Match history and dates, used to power the History tab and the activity graph on Home.
- A roster of players you add on the Players tab (name and date added), used to fill in match setup, rank players on the Leaderboard, and show each player's own activity graph.
- App preferences, such as your Pro/Premium status.

Team and player names are whatever you type in when starting a match or adding a player. Smashly does not verify or require them to be real names, and they never leave your device except when you choose to share an activity image (see **Sharing Your Activity** below) or use the live-watching feature (see **Watching Live Matches** below).

Smashly does **not** collect, store, or transmit:

- Your real name, email address, or phone number (outside of the optional Contact Us email described below)
- Location data
- Contacts
- Browsing history

## In-App Purchases (Smashly Premium)

Smashly offers optional Premium plans (monthly, yearly, and lifetime) for unlimited match tracking, powered by Apple's In-App Purchase system and processed through **RevenueCat**, our subscription management provider.

To manage purchases and restore entitlements, RevenueCat generates an anonymous device identifier (e.g. `$RCAnonymousID:...`) and receives purchase/subscription data from the App Store (such as product purchased, purchase date, and renewal status). This identifier is not linked to your name, email, or any other personal information collected by Smashly.

RevenueCat's handling of this data is governed by [RevenueCat's Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/).

## Voice Commands (Microphone & Speech Recognition)

Smashly optionally lets you score hands-free during a match by saying "Left," "Right," "Undo," or "Score." This requires Microphone and Speech Recognition permission, which iOS will ask for the first time you use this feature (or during onboarding).

- Voice recognition is only active while a match is being played, listening for these specific commands.
- Speech recognition is provided by Apple's Speech framework. Depending on your device and settings, Apple may process this audio on-device or on Apple's servers to convert it to text; Smashly itself does not store, transmit, or have access to your voice recordings or transcripts.
- You can deny or later revoke Microphone/Speech Recognition access in your device Settings — Smashly remains fully usable by tapping to score instead.

## Watching Live Matches (Local Network & Bluetooth)

The "Watch a Live Match" feature lets another nearby iPhone follow your match's live score using a join code, and lets you do the same for someone else's match. This requires Local Network and Bluetooth permission, which iOS will ask for the first time you host or watch a match.

- This feature uses Apple's MultipeerConnectivity framework to connect directly to nearby devices over your local Wi-Fi network and/or Bluetooth — no internet connection, account, or Smashly server is involved.
- While a match is being hosted or watched, the data being tracked (team/player names, scores, and game progress) is sent directly between the connected devices over that local connection, for the duration of the match only.
- Smashly does not collect or store this data anywhere beyond the host device's own match history.

## Sharing Your Activity

The Home tab, and each player's own page on the Players tab, let you generate and share a card showing activity (yours, or that player's). Generating this image requires access to your Photos library (to save the image) and uses the system share sheet (to share it elsewhere).

- The activity image is created and saved entirely on your device.
- Smashly does not upload this image or your underlying match data anywhere — sharing only happens if and when you tap Share or Download, and only to the destination you choose.

## Data Storage

Smashly stores match history and app preferences locally on your device using MMKV, a fast local key-value storage library.

We do not operate servers that collect or store your match data or personal information. Nothing you enter in the app is synced to the cloud or to us.

## Third-Party Services

Smashly does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

The app relies on a small number of third-party and Apple-provided services necessary for its functionality:

- **RevenueCat** — processes in-app purchases and subscription status (see above).
- **Apple** — App Store purchases, Photos access, the native share sheet, Speech Recognition, and MultipeerConnectivity (local network/Bluetooth device discovery), governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Contact Us Feature

The Contact Us option in Settings opens your device's email app with a pre-filled message that includes your device model, OS version, app version, and your anonymous RevenueCat ID — this helps us troubleshoot issues. This information is only sent if you choose to send the email; Smashly does not transmit it automatically.

## Children's Privacy

Smashly does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Since no personal information is collected or transmitted by the app itself, Smashly presents minimal privacy risks to children.

## Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information stored on your device. However, no method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Your Rights

Because Smashly does not collect personal information or maintain user accounts, there is generally no personal data stored by us to access, modify, or delete.

You can remove all app data at any time by:

- Deleting the app, which removes all locally stored match history and preferences.

To manage or cancel a Premium subscription, use the **Manage Subscription** option in Settings or your Apple ID account settings.

### Summary

Smashly stores your match history, player roster, and preferences locally on your device.
