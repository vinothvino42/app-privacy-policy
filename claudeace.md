# Privacy Policy for Claude Ace

**Last Updated: October 16, 2026**

## Privacy Policy

Welcome to Claude Ace. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Claude Ace collects, uses, and protects your information.

## Information We Collect

Claude Ace helps you stay focused by locking distracting apps until you pass a quick round of mock Claude certification questions — all without an account or sign-up.

The app stores the following information locally on your device:

- The apps and categories you choose to block, represented as privacy-preserving tokens provided by Apple's Screen Time APIs (see **Screen Time & App Blocking** below).
- Your practice preferences, such as the certification track you're studying for, the number of questions per round, and how long apps stay unlocked after a passing score.
- Your block/unlock status and timers, used to lock and automatically re-lock your selected apps.
- App preferences, such as your onboarding progress and notification preference.

Claude Ace does **not** collect, store, or transmit:

- Your real name, email address, or phone number (outside of the optional Contact Us email described below)
- Location data
- Contacts
- Browsing history

## Screen Time & App Blocking

Claude Ace uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls, Managed Settings, and Device Activity) to let you select apps and categories to block, and to enforce that block until you pass a practice round.

- The apps and categories you select are represented by opaque tokens that Apple provides. Claude Ace never learns which specific apps or categories these tokens represent — only Apple's system frameworks can resolve them, which is also why the app icons shown in the app list are rendered directly by the system.
- The app extensions that enforce blocking (Shield Configuration, Shield Action, and Device Activity Monitor) run entirely on your device and do not transmit any data off of it.

## Practice Questions

Each practice round draws from a bundled set of mock questions modeled on the real Claude certification exams. These questions ship with the app itself — answering them does not send any data to us or to Anthropic, and your answers, scores, and quiz history are not recorded or transmitted anywhere.

## Notifications

Claude Ace can send you local notifications — for example, to let you know your apps are blocked or that it's time for another practice round. These notifications are generated and scheduled entirely on your device using Apple's notification framework; they are not sent from any server, and no notification content or history is transmitted to us.

## Data Storage

Claude Ace stores your app selection, practice preferences, and app state locally on your device using `UserDefaults`, shared within an App Group so the app and its Screen Time extensions can work together.

We do not operate servers that collect or store your personal information. Nothing you configure in the app is synced to the cloud or to us.

## Third-Party Services

Claude Ace does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

The app relies only on Apple-provided frameworks necessary for its functionality — Screen Time (Family Controls, Managed Settings, Device Activity) and local notifications — governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Claude Ace does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection SDKs.

## Contact Us Feature

The Contact Us option in Settings opens your device's email app with a pre-filled message that includes your device model, OS version, and app version — this helps us troubleshoot issues. This information is only sent if you choose to send the email; Claude Ace does not transmit it automatically.

## Children's Privacy

Claude Ace does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Since no personal information is collected or transmitted by the app itself, Claude Ace presents minimal privacy risks to children.

## Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information stored on your device. However, no method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Your Rights

Because Claude Ace does not collect personal information or maintain user accounts, there is generally no personal data stored by us to access, modify, or delete.

You can remove all app data at any time by:

- Deleting the app, which removes all locally stored preferences, app selections, and state.
